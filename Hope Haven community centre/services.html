Our Services & Programs

  • After-School Program

    Homework help, tutoring and safe space for children aged 12-18. Mon-Fri 14:00 - 17:00.

  • Skills Training

    Cooking, sewing and computer literacy classes for youth aged 16-25 to improve job opportunities.

  • Food Parcels

    Monthly food parcels distributed to 150 families in need within the Pretoria area.

  • Holiday Camps

    Free holiday programs with sports, arts and mentorship during school breaks.
