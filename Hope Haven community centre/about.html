About Us

Our History

Hope Haven was founded in 2018 by a group of teachers who saw children going to school hungry. We started with 10 children and a soup kitchen. Today we support over 300 youth.

Our Mission

To uplift and empower vulnerable communities through education, nutrition, and support services.

Our Vision

A community where every child has access to opportunity and hope.
